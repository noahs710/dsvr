# Torso IK solver pass — design

18 October 2026. Approved in-session. Companion to the
[execution plan](../plans/2026-10-17-dsvr-execution-plan.md) (E5) and the
[no-HMD plan](../plans/2026-10-18-no-hmd-session-plan.md). Vector-based
attacks (E8) get their own spec immediately after this one; they are not
designed here.

## Context

The torso follow slice is headset-qualified: spine-subtree yaw toward the
heading plus clamped head yaw, torso-carried lean, guarded reach scaling.
Its known limits, in the user's words: the torso rotates as one rigid
block; the head bone does not snap to the camera; the spine/pelvis do not
settle at a middle ground between head rotation and hand pulls; and micro
head movements reach the body unfiltered, risking vibration.

## Goals

1. Distribute torso yaw and lean pitch across Spine, Chest and Neck with
   per-joint limits, replacing the single rigid spine rotation.
2. Pelvis takes a bounded share of the torso yaw (legs included), so the
   whole body reads as one posture instead of a twisted upper block.
3. The torso target is the angular middle ground between the head-driven
   yaw and the yaw the two hands pull toward.
4. Head bone snaps to the camera orientation through the neck, capped by
   a neck limit; any excess stays unapplied rather than winding the neck.
5. Torso state is damped with a deadband: small jittery head movements
   never reach the body; real turns stay responsive. No accumulation
   across frames, deaths, menus or rig changes.

Non-goals: root/physics writes, foot IK or footstep prediction (only
head/hands are tracked), true stereo, combat changes, performance work,
changing calibration capture or its B-button fix (which still awaits a
headset confirm and rides along in the next qualification run).

## Design

All math stays in the existing pure header seam (`body_pose_math.h`),
driven from the existing native pose commit in `arm_ik.cpp`. Native poses
are rebuilt by the game every frame; the solver only ever edits the
current frame's palette copy, atomically, and the commit path's existing
ownership re-verification is untouched.

### Solver pass (per committed frame, before arm solving)

1. **Torso target yaw.** Head-driven desired yaw is the heading plus the
   clamped head yaw (existing twist bound 0.8 rad). Hand-pull yaw is the
   heading-relative yaw (about the head position) of the midpoint of the
   two wrist targets; with
   one hand invalid it contributes nothing. Target is the angular middle:
   `head + 0.5 * wrap(hands - head)`, re-clamped to the correction bound
   (0.8 rad from native facing, as shipped after the camera-in-torso fix).
2. **Pelvis share.** Rotate the pelvis subtree (the spine bone's parent,
   derived structurally; today Upper_Root) by 50% of the target
   correction, capped at ±0.40 rad. Legs follow; feet are never wound
   further than the cap.
3. **Distributed yaw.** The other half of the correction (the pelvis
   took the first half, so the shoulders still arrive at the full
   target while the pelvis lags at its middle ground) is split across Spine,
   Chest, Neck in a 45:35:20 ratio with per-joint caps (Spine 0.45,
   Chest 0.35, Neck 0.50 rad). Any amount a capped joint cannot take is
   redistributed once to the uncapped joints in the same ratio; leftover
   beyond all caps is dropped (anatomical limit, arms may reach-limit
   rather than the torso winding).
4. **Lean.** Existing clamped torso translation (0.30 m, calibrated
   profiles only) is unchanged. Head pitch beyond a ±0.15 rad deadband
   adds a forward/back spine pitch: 35% of the excess, capped at
   0.30 rad, split Spine:Chest 60:40 about each joint's model-space
   right axis. Neck pitch is left to the head snap.
5. **Head snap.** After distribution, compute the head bone's world basis
   and rotate the Neck subtree by the residual that makes it equal the
   camera basis, capped at 0.60 rad. The head mesh is hidden; this keeps
   the bone, neck silhouette and any head-worn attachments coherent with
   the view instead of lagging the animated stance.
6. **Arms.** Unchanged: both arms then solve from the corrected
   shoulders with the existing two-bone solver, grip orientation, reach
   guard and two-hand support.

Every stage refuses atomically on malformed rigs/bases (existing
contract): a failed stage leaves the palette at the last good stage, and
a missing spine chain keeps today's pre-torso behavior exactly.

### Smoothing

A small damped state (torso yaw, pelvis yaw, lean vector, spine pitch)
lives on the owner thread in `arm_ik.cpp`, with pure helpers in
`body_pose_math.h`:

- Exponential approach, time constant 90 ms, driven by tracking
  timestamps (clamped 1-100 ms steps). First sample snaps.
- Deadband: target changes under 0.003 rad (yaw/pitch) or 0.002 m (lean)
  hold the previous state, so resting micro-jitter produces a still body.
- Wrap-safe angular approach (shortest arc).
- Reset (snap, no glide) on: rig rebind, calibration reset, menu or
  calibration input capture, missing head basis or spine chain, and
  model-root jumps over 1 m between commits (teleport/load).

### Error handling

Unchanged contracts: no root/physics writes, guarded reads only, fresh
pose/owner re-verification before commit, refusal leaves native
animation untouched. New failure mode is bounded: smoothing state can
only lag the torso by the time constant; it can never accumulate an
offset, because targets are recomputed from native poses every frame.

## Testing

Red-first, in the maintained suites:

- `test_body_pose`: distribution sums to the target with caps and one
  redistribution pass; pelvis fraction and cap; hand-pull middle ground
  including wrap and one-hand cases; pitch deadband/share/cap; head snap
  aligning the basis within the cap and partially beyond it; damping
  step convergence, deadband stillness, wrap safety, timestamp clamps;
  atomic refusal leaves the palette untouched.
- `test_arm_pose_native`: with a bound spine chain and stub camera, the
  committed head bone basis matches the camera basis within tolerance,
  the pelvis basis carries its share, grips still land on target, and
  reversing the head between frames reverses the torso (no accumulation,
  extending the existing check to the damped path).
- Full gate: `tools/auto_verify.ps1` (build, all CTest, doctor, package).

## Headset qualification

One protected SteamVR run reusing the established transaction flow:

- Turn in place and look around: bend looks distributed, not blocky;
  pelvis/legs read as one body; no winding past limits.
- Hold still: body does not vibrate with resting head micro-motion.
- Look down, overhead, lowering: camera stays clear of the chest; arms
  follow, even, wrists fine (regression check on the qualified slice).
- Calibration screen: press B and navigate, then release-then-hold —
  hold still completes (confirms the B-cancel fix riding along).

Performance remains the known open item (~30-44 FPS vs the 90 Hz
target); this design must not make it worse, and stage timings are
compared from the run log before/after.
