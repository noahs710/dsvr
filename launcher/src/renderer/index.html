Required dependency

            
FPS Unlock is required. DSVR will not start without it. It is a separate MIT mod, installed as the chain proxy and hash-verified.

            

              
            

          

          

            
Offline mode

            
No firewall setup is needed. Put Steam in Offline Mode, then set Dark Souls to Offline Mode in the game. Offline Mode makes no online connections until you turn it back on in the game. Texture tools download to Downloads and are never launched by setup.

            

              
            

          

        

        

        
Built with MinHook, OpenXR SDK and Dear ImGui. DSR-FPS-Unlock by zofrea1 is a required dependency, installed separately and hash-verified. Full license notices ship in licenses/.

Controls

          
DSVR's own controls. Combat, movement and menus stay the game's own inputs; these only open DSVR surfaces.

        

        

          
Keyboard

          
    
            
  • HomeOpen or close the DSVR menu
    • 
            
  • F6Recenter the theater view
    • 
            
  • F7Reload DSVR.toml
    • 
            
  • F8Toggle the theater view
    • 
            
  • F9Drop a log marker and start a capture
    • 
          

          
Hotkeys can be disabled in Options. The marker and reload keys are configurable.

        

        

          
In the headset

    • 
            
  • Both sticks clickedhold 3 sOpen or close the DSVR menu
    •

    • 
          
  • Menu+YToggle the theater view
    • 
          
  • Menu+BReload DSVR.toml
    • 
          
  • Menu+XWrite a log marker
    • 
            
  • Left stick click+AtwiceStart calibration
    • 
          

        
Moving and playing

        
    
          
  • Left stickMove. Steered by your head by default, or by the chosen controller hand with head fallback. Hold forward to dash, as in the game.
    • 
          
  • Right stickTurn. Snap-turn jumps the set angle; smooth-turn turns continuously. Snap off means smooth stick turning.
    • 
          
  • Y+weapon-hand shoulderToggle two-hand stance (the game's own toggle; the physical grip attaches first)
    • 
          
  • Squeeze/triggerWeapon hand: light and strong attacks. Off-hand squeeze holds block, flick taps parry. Skill button taps the flourish channel.
    • 
          
  • Stick heldOpens the native D-pad choice instead of walking while held
    • 
          
  • Swing the armsWith arm-swing sprint on, loose alternating arm swings run. A one-hand flap or a two-handed swing reads as combat, not running.
    • 
          
  • Reach forwardoptional, off by defaultWith reach-to-interact on, thrusting one hand into the reach envelope taps the interact button
    • 
        

        
Quest, Index, WMR and simple controller bindings are suggested by the mod. Physical swings, blocks, parries and reaches fire the game's own inputs. Guard and damage outcomes stay the game's.
